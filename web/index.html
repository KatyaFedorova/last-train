LAST TRAIN

Each train has four passengers. One of them is the Agent.

  • The Agent always lies.
  • Everyone else always tells the truth.

Read the four lines. Only one passenger can be the Agent. Tap them or press their letter: A, B, C or D.

Tip: if two passengers contradict each other, one of them is the Agent, so the other two are honest.

You have 1 minute per train, 10 trains per run. The clock starts when you press Start. Right answer: 100 points + 5 per second left. H gives one tip per run.
